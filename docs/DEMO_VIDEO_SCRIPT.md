## ⏱️ Scene-by-Scene Walkthrough Script

### Scene 2: Live AQI & Pollutant Breakdown (0:30 – 0:55)
- **Visual:** Scroll down to the Live AQI Dashboard (`02_live_aqi_dashboard`). Toggle between US EPA and European AQI. Hover over the PM2.5, NO2, and Ozone cards.

  > *"BreatheWise AI is grounded in verified, real-time atmospheric data from Open-Meteo and Copernicus CAMS sensors. ### Scene 3: Diurnal Inversion Timing & Indoor Air Assistant (0:55 – 1:25)
- **Visual:** Click on the **Indoor Hacks** tab (`04_indoor_assistant`). Show the real-time natural ventilation indicator and the 5 practical clinical hacks.
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > *"One of the biggest dilemmas during smog is ventilation. Should you open your windows or keep them sealed? BreatheWise AI models the diurnal smog curve: during cold morning hours from 6 to 10 AM, ground inversion traps toxic smoke, so exterior windows must remain firmly sealed. But between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM, solar radiation breaks the ceiling, creating a safe micro-ventilation window. The app provides 5 zero-cost hacks, like dual-phase kitchen exhaust protocols and DIY Corsi-Rosenthal box air filters."*

---

### Scene 4: Personal Daily Planner & Smart Bio-Dose Calculator (1:25 – 1:55)
- **Visual:** Click on the **Daily Planner** tab (`03_daily_planner`). Select the "Child/Student" profile. Adjust the outdoor hours slider to 2.5 hours and toggle the activity level from "Rest" to "Cardio Run".
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > *"Next is the Personal Daily Planner. Depending on whether you select an Adult, Child, Senior, or Asthma profile, the routine dynamically adapts. Here, our Smart Inhaled Bio-Dose calculator uses the ICRP Human Respiratory Tract Model to calculate the exact micrograms of PM2.5 deposited into the lung alveoli based on breathing rate. It shows that a 2.5-hour outdoor run delivers over 200 µg of fine particulate matter, but wearing a certified N95 respirator eliminates 95% of that exposure."*
