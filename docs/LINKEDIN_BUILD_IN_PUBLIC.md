🚀 Excited to reveal BREATHEWISE AI — Built with Google Antigravity & Gemini for PromptWars Virtual 2026!

Every winter, millions of citizens across Indian cities and industrial basins wake up to a hazardous ceiling of toxic winter smog. AQI monitors surge past 300, 400, or even 500+.

Yet traditional air quality apps only display an alarming number with generic advice. They leave families without actionable answers:
• "Is it safe for my child to play outdoors at 4 PM?"
• "When should we actually open our windows to prevent indoor mold and VOC buildup?"
• "What is the actual particulate mass I inhale during my morning commute?"

To solve this, I participated in Main Challenge 06 ("Urban Air Quality Assistant") in PromptWars Virtual 2026, organized by Google for Developers in collaboration with Hack2skill, and created BREATHEWISE AI ("Understand Your Air. Protect Your Day. Breathe Smarter.").

💡 What makes BreatheWise AI a real decision-support platform?

1. ☀️ Diurnal Inversion Timing: Grounded in atmospheric physics, it alerts users that morning hours (6–10 AM) trap vehicular exhaust within a shallow inversion ceiling, while midday solar convection (1:00–3:30 PM) breaks the lid, offering a safe micro-ventilation window.
2. 🫁 Smart Inhaled Bio-Dose Calculator: Implements the ICRP Human Respiratory Tract Model to calculate the exact mass (µg) of PM2.5 deposited into pulmonary alveoli based on minute ventilation rates (0.5 to 2.5 m³/h) and duration, showing 95% particulate savings with certified N95 respirators.
3. 🚆 Clean Commute Swap Engine: Directly compares Metro Rail, EV cabs, carpooling, solo ICE cars, and cycling—quantifying CO₂ saved and particulate exposure.
4. 🤖 Grounded Gemini AI Copilot: Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, answering lifestyle queries grounded strictly in live environmental sensors (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, O3, CO) without hallucinating numbers.
5. 🛡️ Family Vulnerability Modes: Custom protective guidelines for pediatric lungs, expectant mothers, elderly parents (65+), and asthma/COPD patients.
6. 🌐 Bilingual & Printable: Full English and Hindi (हिंदी) interface with printable daily health advisory cards.

🛠️ Tech Stack:
• Development Environment: Google Antigravity
• Frontend: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4
• Backend: Python 3.12 + FastAPI + Pydantic v2
• GenAI: Google Gemini 2.0 Flash API (google-genai SDK)
• Data: Open-Meteo Air Quality & Copernicus CAMS APIs

Building this with Google Antigravity allowed me to move from concept to a production-ready, fully tested full-stack architecture with rigorous unit/endpoint testing.
